crt-rack · live demo

Your site, but it's rack gear.

Everything on this page is the rack. Bottom rail: VFD clock, live ticker, scroll position in dB, real LCP/CLS from your browser. Press ⌘K / Ctrl+K for the command palette. Hit the ⏻ power button bottom-left for the full CRT shutdown-and-reboot.

Scroll me

The dB readout tracks your position. Real PerformanceObserver numbers in the HUD - not fake ones.

Zero dependencies

One CSS file, one JS file, ~10KB combined. No framework, no build step, no analytics. The ticker data comes from a 40-line PHP proxy with a 30-minute cache.

Graceful everywhere

No PHP? The ticker falls back to a static line. Reduced motion? The CRT animation steps aside. Mobile? The HUD hides, the rail stays.
